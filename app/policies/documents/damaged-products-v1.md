## 1. Reporting window
Items that arrive damaged must be reported within 7 days of delivery. Reports made later are reviewed by a specialist.

## 2. Information required
To assess a damage claim, support needs a description of the damage and confirmation that the customer can provide photos of the item and packaging.

## 3. Resolution options
For damage reported within the window with a description and photos, the customer may receive a full refund of the order amount, subject to reviewer approval, or a replacement. Replacements are arranged by the fulfilment team, not by automated support.

## 4. Claims without evidence
If the customer cannot provide photos, the claim is referred to a specialist for assessment.
