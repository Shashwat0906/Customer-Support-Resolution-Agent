## 1. Scope
This policy applies to orders delivered on or after 1 July 2026. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (INR). It replaces version 1.0 of the refund eligibility policy for those orders.

## 2. Return window
A return must be requested within 21 days of the delivery date. Returns requested after 21 days are not eligible for a refund under this policy.

## 3. When a refund becomes eligible
Refunds for returned items become eligible after the return is received and inspection passes. Items that are in transit back to us, awaiting inspection, rejected at the warehouse, or that failed inspection are not eligible yet or at all.

## 4. Refund amount
The refund equals the amount paid for the order, less any amount already refunded or currently being refunded. A refund is never issued twice for the same order amount.

## 5. Approval
A support reviewer must approve every refund. Refunds above INR 30,000 require approval by a senior reviewer. An approval applies only to the exact refund that was reviewed; if the order, amount or eligibility changes, a new approval is required.
