## 1. Starting a return
Customers start a return from their order page. A return pickup is scheduled within two business days.

## 2. Receiving and inspection
Returned items are inspected within three business days of arriving at the warehouse. Inspection checks that the item and its accessories are complete and free of customer-caused damage.

## 3. Inspection outcomes
An inspection either passes, fails, or is referred for human review. Failed inspections and rejected returns are explained to the customer with the reason recorded by the warehouse.

## 4. Refund timing
After an approved refund is issued, the payment provider usually credits the original payment method within five to seven business days. Support should only tell a customer that a refund was issued once the payment provider has confirmed it.
