## 1. Tracking information
Support shares the latest tracking status reported by the carrier, including the estimated delivery date. Support must not promise a delivery date the carrier has not provided.

## 2. Minor delays
A shipment up to 3 days past its estimated delivery date is treated as a minor delay. Support explains the latest carrier status and the customer does not need to take action.

## 3. Significant delays
A shipment more than 3 days past its estimated delivery date is escalated to the logistics team, who contact the carrier and may offer a cancellation and refund.

## 4. Carrier exceptions
If the carrier reports an exception such as damage in transit or a lost parcel, the case is escalated to the logistics team immediately.
