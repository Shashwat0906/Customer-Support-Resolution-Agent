## 1. Scope
This policy applies to orders delivered between 1 January 2024 and 30 June 2026. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (INR). It covers refunds for items returned by the customer.

## 2. Return window
A return must be requested within 30 days of the delivery date. Returns requested after 30 days are not eligible for a refund under this policy.

## 3. When a refund becomes eligible
Refunds for returned items become eligible after the return is received at the VoltCart warehouse and the inspection passes. A return that is still in transit, awaiting inspection, rejected, or that failed inspection is not eligible for a refund.

## 4. Refund amount
The refund equals the amount paid for the returned order, less any amount already refunded. Partial returns of multi-item orders are handled by a specialist and are out of scope for automated handling.

## 5. Approval
A support reviewer must approve every refund before it is issued. Refunds above INR 30,000 require a senior reviewer.
