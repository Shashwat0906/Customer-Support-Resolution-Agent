## 1. General rule
Automated support must hand a case to a human whenever the applicable policy cannot be established, policies conflict, or the facts are uncertain.

## 2. Mandatory escalations
The following always require human review: inspection results marked for review, refunds where the payment provider and the VoltCart ledger disagree, orders that predate every published policy version, carrier exceptions, and any refund outcome that cannot be confirmed with the payment provider.

## 3. Out of scope
Chargebacks, fraud investigations and partial returns of multi-item orders are always handled by specialists.

## 4. Customer communication
When a case is escalated, tell the customer what was verified, that a specialist will follow up, and do not promise an outcome.
